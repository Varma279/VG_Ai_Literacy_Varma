# Week 04 — Sources and References

This file records the learning resources and research papers used to understand deep learning, neural networks, model training and their applications in VLSI Physical Design.

## 1. Google Machine Learning Crash Course

**Source:** Google Developers — Machine Learning Crash Course

**Topics supported:**

* Numerical data and feature representation.
* Data preparation and normalisation.
* Neural networks and their components.
* Training, overfitting and generalisation.

**Why I used it:** To understand the fundamentals of machine learning and deep learning.

## 2. TensorFlow Neural Network Playground

**Source:** TensorFlow — Neural Network Playground

**Topics supported:**

* Input features and hidden layers.

* Effects of changing network complexity.

**Why I used it:** To perform Mission 5 and visually explore how neural network configurations affect classification.

## 3. Neural Networks for Routing Congestion Prediction in VLSI Design Using Initial Layout Parameters (2025)

**Authors:** M. Kh. Saibodalov, M. V. Dashiev, I. Karandashev, N. V. Zheludkov and E. S. Kocheva.

**Publication:** Proceedings of ISP RAS, Volume 37, Issue 3, 2025, Pages 9–18.

**DOI:** 10.15514/ISPRAS-2025-37(3)-1

**Topics supported:**

* Neural-network-based routing congestion prediction.
* Early congestion prediction during VLSI layout design.
* Using initial layout parameters to predict congestion maps.
* Potential benefits for floorplanning and macro placement decisions.

**Why I used it:** To support the engineering case study on predicting routing congestion before detailed routing.

## 4. Leveraging Steiner Tree Grid Segment in GNN for VLSI Pre-Routing Congestion Prediction (2025)

**Authors:** Xuan Peng et al.

**Publication:** 2025 International Symposium of Electronics Design Automation (ISEDA).

**DOI:** 10.1109/ISEDA65950.2025.11100984

**Topics supported:**

* Graph Neural Networks for routing congestion prediction.
* Steiner tree-based graph representation.
* Classification of routing overflow congestion.
* Evaluation of congestion prediction using F1-score.

**Why I used it:** To understand how graph neural networks can be applied to pre-routing congestion prediction.

## 5. Verification and Limitations

The Google resources support the fundamental concepts discussed in Missions 1–4. TensorFlow Playground supports the practical exploration in Mission 5.

The two research papers demonstrate that neural-network-based and graph-neural-network-based approaches have been investigated for VLSI routing congestion prediction.

The engineering case study is a proposed application based on these research directions. It should not be interpreted as an implementation completed by me or as proof that all commercial EDA tools use these approaches.

## Conclusion

These references helped connect the fundamentals of deep learning with a practical engineering application. They also helped distinguish general deep learning concepts from research demonstrations in VLSI Physical Design.
