9: Write 5 Rules for Using AI Responsibly

## My Five Rules

1. **Verify Important Information:** Always check AI-generated facts using reliable sources before accepting them.

2. **Protect Personal Data:** Never share passwords, private information, or confidential company data with AI tools.

3. **Understand Before Using:** Read and understand AI-generated answers instead of copying them without knowing their meaning.

4. **Check for Mistakes:** AI can produce incorrect or misleading information, so review its responses carefully.

5. **Take Human Responsibility:** Use AI as a supporting tool, but make important decisions based on your own knowledge and proper verification.

## What I Learned

I learned that AI is useful for learning and solving problems, but it must be used carefully. Verifying information, protecting privacy, and applying human judgment help ensure responsible AI use.
