
5: Ask an AI a Question You Can Verify, and Check Whether It Was Right

## 1. Prompt

When was the first artificial satellite, Sputnik 1, launched, and which country launched it?

## 2. AI Answer

The AI explained that Sputnik 1 was launched on 4 October 1957 by the Soviet Union. It was the first artificial satellite to orbit Earth and marked an important milestone in the history of space exploration.

## 3. Verification Sources

1. NASA: https://www.nasa.gov/history/sputnik/
2. Encyclopaedia Britannica: https://www.britannica.com/technology/Sputnik-satellite

I used these sources to check the launch date, the country responsible for the launch, and the historical significance of Sputnik 1.

## 4. Result

The answer was correct based on the sources I checked. They confirm that Sputnik 1 was launched by the Soviet Union on 4 October 1957 and became the first artificial satellite to orbit Earth.

## 5. What My Test Proved and Did Not Prove

My test showed that the AI provided accurate historical information for this particular question. However, one correct answer does not prove that every AI-generated response is reliable. Other claims must be checked independently.

## 6. Lesson Learned

I learned that AI can provide useful explanations, but its answers should not automatically be treated as facts. Checking reliable sources helps confirm information and reduces the risk of accepting incorrect claims.
