8: How AI Can Help in My VLSI Track

## Chosen Track: Physical Design (PD)

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Task** | Analysing congestion reports to identify areas with routing difficulties. |
| **How AI might help** | AI could analyse congestion data, highlight crowded regions, and suggest possible improvements to placement or routing. |
| **Why human knowledge still matters** | A physical design engineer must evaluate timing, area, and routing constraints before applying any suggested changes. |

## What I Learned

I learned that AI could help PD engineers identify routing congestion and explore possible solutions faster. However, engineers must verify the suggestions to ensure the design meets its physical and timing requirements.
